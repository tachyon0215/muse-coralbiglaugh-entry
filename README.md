# muse-coralbiglaugh-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [One Line, Big Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-big-laugh-328fb857).

## The entry

> I asked Alexa to lower my stress and she dimmed the lights, played ocean sounds, and ordered a coffin catalogue 'just to browse'.

Five candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: Escalating helpfulness curdles into a specific, quotable dark turn: lights, ocean sounds, then a coffin catalogue 'just to browse' - a real joke with misdirection and an absurdly specific detail that stays in the head.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 bf3ef2b4fbc709b3ed9017b285711b214568c30dfcb6bfddcd476c3b31e4eaba).
